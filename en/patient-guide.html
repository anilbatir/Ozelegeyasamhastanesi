PATIENT GUIDE

We're here to help

A short guide covering the whole journey, from booking your appointment to leaving the hospital. If anything is unclear, don't hesitate to call our call center.

STEP BY STEP

How to book an appointment

1

Choose your department and doctor

Browse our Services page to find the department that fits your needs, among our 19 specialties.

2

Request by phone or online form

Call +90 232 226 56 56 or fill out our online appointment form.

3

Our team calls to confirm

Your request is reviewed, and our team contacts you to confirm the date and time.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

What to bring with you

Bring a valid ID (national ID card, driving licence or passport).

Bring your SGK or private health insurance documents if you have them; call our call center for up-to-date information on our partner insurers.

Arriving 10-15 minutes before your appointment speeds up check-in.

Bring any previous test results, imaging or medical reports, if available.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Call our call center at 0232 226 56 56 to cancel your appointment or move it to a new date.

Which departments can I book an appointment with?

We provide care across 19 medical specialties. You can see the full list on our Services page.

What should I do in an emergency?

Our Emergency unit operates 24/7. You can come directly to our hospital, or call 112 in a life-threatening situation.

Does the online appointment form guarantee a confirmed appointment?

Appointment requests submitted through the online form are reviewed by our team, who will contact you to confirm the date and time.

I have health insurance / a partner institution — do you accept it?

We work with SGK and many private health insurers. You can see our partner institutions on our homepage, and call our call center for details on your specific policy.

What are the visiting hours?

Visiting hours can vary by ward; please check with the nursing desk at the hospital or call our call center for current information.
