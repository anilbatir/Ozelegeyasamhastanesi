Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

We process personal data in accordance with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"). This page explains how.

1. Data Controller

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), your personal data may be processed by Özel Ege Yaşam Hastanesi (Ali Fuat Cebesoy, 9135/4. Sk. No:4, 35160 Karabağlar/İzmir, Türkiye) acting as data controller, within the scope described below.

2. Personal Data We Process

Through the appointment form on our website, we process identity and contact data such as your full name, phone number, preferred department and date, and any message you choose to share. If you visit our hospital in person, your health data is additionally processed in accordance with applicable healthcare legislation, including patient rights and personal health data regulations.

3. Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed to receive and manage your appointment requests, to contact you, to carry out patient registration and healthcare service processes, to fulfil our legal obligations, and to improve the quality of our hospital services.

4. Legal Basis

Your personal data is processed on the legal bases set out in Articles 5 and 6 of the KVKK, including your explicit consent, direct connection to the establishment or performance of a contract, fulfilment of a legal obligation, and the protection of public health.

5. Transfer of Personal Data

Your personal data may be shared, to the extent legally required, with authorised public institutions (e.g. the Ministry of Health, the Social Security Institution) and, where the service requires it, with our partner private insurance companies, in accordance with the conditions set out in the KVKK.

6. Retention Period

Your personal data is retained for as long as required by the purpose of processing and the retention periods set out in applicable legislation, in particular healthcare legislation on patient records; at the end of these periods it is deleted, destroyed, or anonymised in accordance with the law.

7. Your Rights Under Article 11 of the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is being processed; request information about such processing; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose; know the third parties to whom your data is transferred, domestically or abroad; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction of your data within the conditions set out in the KVKK; and object to a result that is to your detriment arising from the exclusive analysis of your data through automated systems.

8. How to Apply

To exercise the rights listed above, you can send your request to iletisim@egeyasamhastanesi.com.tr or in writing to our hospital's address above.

9. Cookie Policy

Our website loads font files from Google Fonts so that pages display correctly; while doing so, your browser may connect to Google's servers. Our site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies to track user behaviour. If this changes in the future, this page will be updated accordingly.
